# Local SEO roadmap

## Position and research

Primary market: Bunbury. Secondary market: South West WA, with visit availability confirmed per enquiry. The main commercial offer is an onsite **Cyber Security Check-Up**, not a certified audit or ongoing managed security service.

An initial public-search review on 13 October 2026 found local assessment and broader IT/security offerings. [South West Onsite Computers](https://onsitecomputers.com.au/cyber-security/) presents a NIST-oriented assessment and report alongside security bundles. The opportunity inferred for CyberSafe SW is to make a personally delivered visit, understandable findings and the tangible report especially clear to homes, sole traders and smaller businesses. This is a positioning judgement, not evidence of market share, search volume or ranking difficulty.

Candidate terms from the brief are mapped below. Validate their actual demand using Search Console after launch and an appropriate keyword tool if desired. Do not invent volume or treat early average positions as reliable rankings. The homepage itself is the dedicated **Cyber Security Bunbury** landing page; a near-duplicate `/cyber-security-bunbury` page would compete with it and has deliberately not been created.

## Implemented technical foundation

- Static HTML with crawlable main content and one h1 on each page.
- Unique titles/descriptions, non-www HTTPS canonical URLs, OG and Twitter tags with real PNG assets.
- Organization and WebSite JSON-LD; accurate Service records on service pages; Article records and timestamps; BreadcrumbList records.
- No fabricated street address, ratings, prices, hours, qualifications or review schema. LocalBusiness address-based markup is deferred until accurate information supports it.
- 20 indexable content URLs in sitemap.xml. robots.txt points to the production-domain sitemap. The custom 404 is noindex.
- Useful internal links among services, audience pages, articles and the report. Quote buttons carry service selection to contact.
- Lightweight CSS/JS, SVG graphics, no remote fonts or client framework, sensible caching and security headers.

Schema syntax and page structure are checked locally. Google eligibility and interpretation require deployed validation. Structured data never guarantees a rich result, and FAQ content is for users rather than a rich-result promise. See [Google sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap).

## Before launch

Confirm actual service coverage, business hours and contact details. Complete and verify contact@cybersafesw.com. Phone is owner-supplied. Decide whether the site launches phone-only or with the tested email function. Review privacy handling and retention. Set analytics only if desired, verify consent and preserve the exclusion of personal details from events. Review all commercial copy and metadata. Check sitemap, canonical URLs, HTTPS, status codes, redirects and deployed mobile performance.

Prepare a genuine Google Business Profile. Confirm eligibility, research an accurate available category, supply the real business name, contact method and service area, and complete Google's verification process. Use real photographs. Do not invent a public storefront. If customers are not received at the business address, follow Google's service-area guidance and hide the address as required. Coverage should reflect actual service availability. See [Google service-area guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481).

## First 30 days

1. Verify a Search Console domain property and submit the sitemap.
2. Inspect the homepage, assessment and two audience landing pages. Monitor indexing and canonical selection.
3. Fix response-code, rendering, crawl or security-header problems.
4. Verify lead events against actual provider-accepted enquiries. Keep phone clicks separate from real calls.
5. Review impressions, relevant queries and landing pages; expect limited data at first.
6. Inspect index coverage for the six supporting service pages and article hub.
7. Check the Business Profile website link and contact information for accuracy.

## Days 30–90

Publish useful content when real customer questions justify it. Review Search Console queries and improve pages whose message does not match visitor intent. Keep advice accurate and reviewed against primary guidance. Add relevant internal links. Build genuine local citations with consistent business name, contact and domain. Request authentic feedback after completed work; add real reviews or case studies only with suitable permission. Review enquiry quality rather than page visits alone.

## Long term

Maintain useful educational content, refresh dated platform instructions and review keyword opportunities. Develop local relationships and real customer evidence. Improve conversion paths using actual observations. Audit technical SEO after major changes. Add geographic pages only for confirmed coverage and genuinely different useful content; do not clone suburb pages. Maintain domain/hosting ownership and recovery access.

## Monthly reporting checklist

Use a short spreadsheet or dashboard with reporting month, comparison period and explanatory notes:

- Search Console organic clicks, impressions, CTR and average position (with the metric's limitations).
- Relevant queries grouped by service and audience; identify unrelated traffic.
- Top commercial landing pages and their query mix.
- Genuine form successes, telephone clicks and separately recorded qualified enquiries; avoid double-counting.
- Indexing, canonical, HTTPS, 404 and other technical issues.
- Mobile/desktop split and consent-aware analytics coverage.
- Content gaps from actual questions and query patterns.
- Actions completed, results observed and the next month's priorities.

No analytics or ranking data exists in this project. Success should be assessed from real customer enquiries and business outcomes after launch, not invented dashboard numbers.
